本隐私政策是《最终用户许可协议》（以下简称"EULA"）的组成部分，
说明本软件如何处理您的数据。凡涉及数据处理的事项，如本政策与
EULA 存在冲突，以本政策为准。

1. 概览

    本软件以本地使用为主，不在本地设备之外收集、存储或传输任何
    个人数据。仅当您提交反馈或在 Steam 平台启用云同步时，相关
    数据才会离开您的设备（见第 2(c)、3 条）。

2. 存储的数据

    (a) 游戏存档（宠物、装扮、成就、设置等），存储于本地
        user:// 目录；
    (b) 用户生成内容（UGC，包括部件图片与自定义文案），存储于
        本地 user://ugc_custom/ 与 user://custom_talk/ 目录，
        不上传、不同步；
    (c) 当您在 Steam 平台运行本软件并启用 Steam 云同步时，游戏
        存档（可能包含您在使用过程中主动提供的宠物名字、昵称、
        生日等信息）将经由 Steam 云服务在您本人的设备之间同步，
        用于保留桌宠对您的记忆。同步数据存储于 Valve 提供的
        Steam 服务器，仅对您的 Steam 账号可见，开发者无法读取。
        您可在 Steam 客户端「设置 → 云」中随时关闭云同步；关闭
        后存档仅保留在本地，不再同步。
    您可随时删除本地存档文件夹以清除上述数据。

3. 反馈表单

    本软件设置页面的「游戏反馈」链接会打开由第三方工具 Tally
    提供的在线表单（https://tally.so/r/gDXzPN）。您在该表单中
    填写的任何内容（可能包括您的联系方式）将由 Tally 及其关联方
    处理，不经过开发者服务器。建议您在提交前查看 Tally 的隐私政策。

4. 分析、追踪与遥测

    本软件不包含分析、追踪或遥测功能，不收集使用行为统计数据。

5. 第三方服务

    本软件可能包含第三方组件与外部链接。第三方组件清单及许可
    信息见 THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt。您选择接入的反馈表单服务商
    的隐私实践不在开发者控制范围内。

6. 未成年人

    本软件面向已满 13 周岁的用户。未成年人使用本软件前应在法定
    监护人陪同下阅读并同意本政策及 EULA。

7. 政策变更

    如本政策发生实质性变更，开发者将按照 EULA 第 8.2 条的方式
    通知您。

8. 联系我们

========================================
   Desktop Mochipal — Privacy Policy
========================================

Version 1.1 · Effective Date: August 4, 2026

This privacy policy is an integral part of the End User License
Agreement ("EULA") and explains how the Software handles your data. In
the event of any conflict between this policy and the EULA regarding
data processing matters, this policy shall prevail.

1. Overview

   The Software is primarily used locally and does not collect, store,
   or transmit any personal data outside your local device. Data leaves
   your device only when you submit feedback or enable cloud
   synchronization on the Steam platform (see Sections 2(c) and 3).

2. Data Stored

   (a) Game save data (pets, customizations, achievements, settings,
       etc.), stored locally in the user:// directory;
   (b) User-Generated Content (UGC, including decal images and custom
       text), stored in the local user://ugc_custom/ and
       user://custom_talk/ directories, and neither uploaded nor
       synchronized;
   (c) When you run the Software on the Steam platform and enable Steam
       Cloud synchronization, your game save (which may include
       information you voluntarily provide while using the Software,
       such as pet names, nicknames, and birthdays) will be synchronized
       between your own devices via Steam Cloud services, which is used
       to preserve the desktop pet's memories of you. Synchronized data
       is stored on Valve's Steam servers, is visible only to your
       Steam account, and cannot be read by the Developer. You may turn
       off cloud synchronization at any time in the Steam client under
       "Settings → Cloud"; once disabled, your save data remains local
       only and is no longer synchronized.
   You may delete the local save folder at any time to clear the
   above data.

3. Feedback Form

   The "Game Feedback" link on the Software's settings page opens an
   online form provided by the third-party tool Tally
   (https://tally.so/r/gDXzPN). Any content you enter in that form
   (which may include your contact information) is processed by Tally
   and its affiliates and does not pass through the Developer's
   servers. We recommend reviewing Tally's privacy policy before
   submitting.

4. Analytics, Tracking, and Telemetry

   The Software does not include analytics, tracking, or telemetry
   features and does not collect usage statistics.

5. Third-Party Services

   The Software may contain third-party components and external links.
   The list of third-party components and their license information can
   be found in THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt. The privacy practices of the
   feedback form provider you choose to connect to are outside the
   Developer's control.

6. Minors

   The Software is intended for users who are at least 13 years of age.
   Minors should read and agree to this policy and the EULA together
   with their legal guardian.

7. Changes to This Policy

   If this policy undergoes material changes, the Developer will notify
   you in the manner set forth in Section 8.2 of the EULA.

8. Contact Us

   If you have any privacy-related questions, please contact us through
   the channels set forth in Section 11 of the EULA.
